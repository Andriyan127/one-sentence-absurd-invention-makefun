# Candidate pitches — One-Sentence Absurd Invention Pitch

Competition: `u-suroso-69-one-sentence-absurd-invention-pitch-5326c34a`
(sponsor suroso, make.fun). Judging: a house judge picks the better of two
texts per call, 3 Swiss rounds vs other entries + 3 fixed house texts, then a
playoff among the top 12. Criteria: how funny the invention is, how clever
the fix is, how original the idea is.

Bar to beat (entries already submitted): a microwave that whispers "your food
is ready" in a librarian's voice and then shushes itself; "Sneeze Escrow
Service"; "The Guilt-Tripping Fridge Light". Microwaves, fridges and sneeze
ideas were therefore avoided — every candidate below uses a fresh everyday
annoyance and a fresh absurd mechanism.

Scoring: funny invention /10, clever fix /10, originality /10, total /30.

## 1. BlameShift Umbrella — WINNER — 27/30 (164 characters)

> Behold the BlameShift Umbrella: it opens itself indoors, blames the forecast for your bad hair day, then files a formal complaint against the clouds on your behalf.

- Funny invention: 9/10 — an umbrella that sues the weather is an escalating absurd image (opens indoors, then files paperwork against clouds).
- Clever fix: 9/10 — the tiny annoyance is a ruined hair day after a wrong forecast; the fix redirects blame and outsources the complaint entirely.
- Originality: 9/10 — no umbrella, weather-blame or formal-complaint mechanism among the visible entries; the bureaucracy-vs-nature twist is fresh.

## 2. Doormat of Denial — 25/30 (142 characters)

> Meet the Doormat of Denial: it politely tells delivery drivers you're out, so you never have to put on pants just to sign for a package again.

- Funny invention: 9/10 — a lying doormat is instantly visual and relatable.
- Clever fix: 8/10 — solves the real annoyance of answering the door half-dressed, but the fix is evasion rather than a mechanism.
- Originality: 8/10 — fresh object (doormat), but "avoiding people at the door" is a familiar joke shape.

## 3. Self-Aware Charger Cable — 24/30 (129 characters)

> The Self-Aware Charger Cable untangles itself, apologises for dying at 2%, and sends your phone an invoice for emotional damages.

- Funny invention: 8/10 — the invoice-for-emotional-damages punchline lands well.
- Clever fix: 8/10 — self-untangling plus an apology directly answers two real cable annoyances.
- Originality: 8/10 — a sentient cable is a known trope; the billing twist is what saves it.

## 4. Passive-Aggressive Toaster — 22/30 (162 characters)

> Introducing the Passive-Aggressive Toaster: it burns one side of your bread, then leaves a tiny note saying 'maybe if you watched me, I wouldn't have to do this'.

- Funny invention: 8/10 — the guilt-tripping note is funny, but close in spirit to the already-entered "Guilt-Tripping Fridge Light".
- Clever fix: 7/10 — it does not actually fix burnt toast; it just complains about it.
- Originality: 7/10 — weakest of the four: passive-aggressive appliances and guilt-tripping kitchen gear are already on the board.

## Why the winner won

The BlameShift Umbrella scored highest on all three criteria at once: the
funniest escalating image (indoor opening → blaming the forecast → suing the
clouds), the cleverest fix (it does not fight the rain, it files a complaint
about it), and the most original mechanism (weather bureaucracy). It is also
safely inside the gate at exactly one sentence and 164 characters (1–220).
